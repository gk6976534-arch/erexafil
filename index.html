Erexafil Ingredients

Erexafil™ is marketed as a men's wellness supplement. When reviewing its ingredients, it is important to understand what each ingredient is intended to support, what scientific evidence exists, and whether the formula's claims are backed by reliable research.

The exact ingredients and quantities should be confirmed using Erexafil's official product label. I don't want to attribute unverified ingredients to the product or provide inaccurate information in your product content.

1

Horny Goat Weed Extract

A traditional botanical commonly used in men's wellness supplements. It is included in Erexafil™ for its marketed role in supporting libido and vitality, although strong clinical evidence for this specific product is lacking.
2

Ashwagandha Root Extract

Ashwagandha is an herb traditionally used in Ayurvedic practices. It has been studied for stress management and other aspects of well-being, but its effects vary depending on the preparation and dosage.
3

Muira Puama Root

Muira puama is a plant traditionally used in South American herbal preparations. It is marketed in some men's supplements for vitality and libido, but reliable clinical evidence remains limited.
4

Mucuna Pruriens Seed Extract

Mucuna pruriens, also known as velvet bean, contains naturally occurring L-DOPA. It has been studied for its effects on dopamine-related pathways and requires caution because it can interact with medications.
5

Ginkgo Biloba Leaf Extract

Ginkgo biloba is a botanical studied for circulation and cognitive function. It can increase bleeding risk when combined with certain medications, including blood thinners.
6

Tribulus Terrestris Fruit Extract

Tribulus terrestris is commonly included in men's wellness formulas. It is traditionally marketed for libido and vitality, but evidence that it reliably increases testosterone in healthy men is insufficient.
7

Korean Ginseng Root Extract

Korean ginseng, also known as Panax ginseng, is traditionally used to support energy and general vitality. Some studies have examined its potential effects on fatigue and sexual function, but results are not conclusive for every use.
8

DHEA

DHEA is a hormone precursor naturally produced by the body. It is sometimes included in hormone-related supplements, but it can affect hormone levels and may cause adverse effects. Medical advice is important before using products containing DHEA.
9

Boron Citrate Complex

Boron is a trace element involved in several biological processes. It has been investigated for its possible relationship with bone health and hormone metabolism, but evidence for testosterone-boosting benefits remains limited.
10

Zinc

Zinc is an essential mineral that supports immune function, normal reproductive function, and the maintenance of normal testosterone levels in the blood. Erexafil's published description lists 11 mg per serving.
